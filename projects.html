ASCENT

Co-principal investigator

Spanish State Research Agency

Advanced simulation codes for electromagnetic thrusters

A coordinated research programme to improve plasma simulation methods and analyze propulsion technologies and alternative propellants.

My research focus

  • Numerical methods for hybrid kinetic–fluid plasma models.
  • Applications to Hall and radiofrequency ion thrusters.
  • Molecular propellants and associated propulsion concepts.

UC3M co-PIs: Eduardo Ahedo and Jiewei Zhou. Coordinated with Adrián Domínguez-Vázquez at the University of Málaga.

Water-vapour EP

Principal investigator

European Space Agency

Water-vapour electric propulsion system

Research on water as a propellant for electric propulsion, connecting molecular plasma chemistry to the analysis of thruster operation and performance.

My research focus

  • Reaction mechanisms and collision data for molecular plasmas.
  • Plasma models for water-vapour thruster operation.
  • Simulation support for propulsion-system development.

ALBATOR

Plume-modeling research

Horizon Europe · EIC Pathfinder

Ion-beam technology for active debris removal

ECR-based multicharged ion beams for active debris removal and other remediation strategies. My contributions focus on the numerical modeling of plasma beams and their interactions with downstream objects.

My research focus

  • EP2PLUS development for plume–object interactions.
  • Momentum transfer and plasma interaction with debris geometries.
  • Co-supervision of Omar Eladarousy’s PhD with Mario Merino.

UC3M principal investigator: Mario Merino.
